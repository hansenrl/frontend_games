Oops, give it another try!

What happens when an answer is wrong, at every grade and in every look.

  1. 1First try

    Pick or type an answer. There is no clock in K–2, so he can count on his fingers for as long as he likes.

  2. 2Oops!

    Oops, give it another try! The wrong meteor wobbles and fades out, so the next try has fewer choices. A typed answer just clears.

  3. 3A picture helps

    The problem is drawn: dots, a ten-frame, tens and ones, rows of eggs or a pie. In Crayon and Clay above, this is what appears after the miss.

  4. 4Rex helps

    After a second miss, Rex works it out with him step by step, and he still gets to tap the answer himself. He earns the star either way.

Nothing is ever taken away. No lives, no game over, and no streak that drops back to zero. Stars only go up, and the star jar fills a little with every problem finished.
