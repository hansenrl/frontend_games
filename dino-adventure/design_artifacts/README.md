# Design artifacts: The Runaway Egg

Planning for a choose-your-own dinosaur adventure: illustrated scenes, a few lines of story,
and 2–3 choices per page. Open the HTML file in a browser.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `round1-story-and-art-styles.html` | Four story ideas, a scene-by-scene plan for *The Runaway Egg* (cast, what the story remembers, five endings), and six animated art-style mockups |
| `story-ideas.md` | All four story ideas and the original Runaway Egg plan, saved to come back to |

## Art styles in round 1

Each mockup shows a different scene from the plan, drawn live with canvas code. Click a choice to see a character's reaction.

1. **Papercraft** (Scene 1, The Runaway Egg): cut-outs with white edges in a layered diorama.
2. **Cel-shaded** (Scene 4, Wobbly River): bold outlines and flat two-tone shading.
3. **Ink & wash folklore** (Scene 9, Mama Rexie): ink lines, cross-hatching, muted print palette, comic panels. Inspired by Joe Todd-Stanton's Brownstone's Mythical Collection.
4. **Watercolour picture book** (Scene 7, Firefly Camp).
5. **Clay stop-motion** (Scene 4, Whispering Forest): 12 fps with a little jitter.
6. **Pixel adventure** (Scene 2, Grandpa Longneck): low-res canvas with a fixed palette.

## Decisions

- **Story:** The Runaway Egg. The other three ideas are kept in `story-ideas.md` for later.
- **Art style:** Papercraft (matches the shelf's home page and Choo-Choo Junction).
- **Reader:** a 5–6 year old reading on their own with a grown-up nearby. Short lines (12 words max),
  one idea per line, tap-to-hear big words, and an optional read-aloud button.
- Changes from the plan while building:
  - "Toot the lullaby" became "Sing the egg song", which is learned at night camp.
  - The glow worms can become friends too, so the forest path can also reach the parade ending.
  - Mama T. rex gets her own "So THAT was the rumble!" page before the hatching.
  - The buddy shortcuts are one page each, so they're a bit quicker.
- Round of feedback after the first build:
  - No emoji anywhere in the game: text buttons, small drawn icons for the pouch, and the
    endings sticker book shows a little picture of each ending.
  - No sound effects. Read-aloud (the device's voice) stays.
  - Pip's crest goes **HONK**, not "toot". Parasaurolophus crests probably made a low, horn-like honk.
