# Design artifacts: The Runaway Egg

Planning for a choose-your-own dinosaur adventure: illustrated scenes, a few lines of story,
and 2–3 choices per page. Open the HTML file in a browser.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `round1-story-and-art-styles.html` | Four story ideas, a scene-by-scene plan for *The Runaway Egg* (cast, what the story remembers, five endings), and six animated art-style mockups |

## Art styles in round 1

Each mockup shows a different scene from the plan, drawn live with canvas code. Click a choice to see a character's reaction.

1. **Papercraft** (Scene 1, The Runaway Egg): cut-outs with white edges in a layered diorama.
2. **Cel-shaded** (Scene 4, Wobbly River): bold outlines and flat two-tone shading.
3. **Ink & wash folklore** (Scene 9, Mama Rexie): ink lines, cross-hatching, muted print palette, comic panels. Inspired by Joe Todd-Stanton's Brownstone's Mythical Collection.
4. **Watercolour picture book** (Scene 7, Firefly Camp).
5. **Clay stop-motion** (Scene 4, Whispering Forest): 12 fps with a little jitter.
6. **Pixel adventure** (Scene 2, Grandpa Longneck): low-res canvas with a fixed palette.
