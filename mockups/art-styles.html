Endurance Docking

A top-down, two-player docking game. Player one flies the Ranger with W A S D; player two handles spin with K and L. Each mockup below is drawn in a different art style and shows a different stage of the docking sequence, so you can judge both the look and the flow.

Four looks

A · Stage shown: Approach

Cinematic

Closest to the film. Grey hull panels lit by a fixed sun so each module brightens as it swings round, an icy planet below, and a live explosion venting sparks and smoke from the two missing modules.

Feel
Tense, quiet, beautiful
HUD
Thin corner text, indicators stay dark until earned
Kid fit
Good, but small text; ship is low contrast
B · Stage shown: Match spin

Flight Plan

Line art on a blueprint grid. A dashed ghost Ranger shows exactly where the ship needs to point, which makes the spin player's job obvious. Dotted trail and live readouts teach what “matching” means.

Feel
Clever, technical, readable
HUD
Boxed readouts with a centre-target spin meter
Kid fit
Great for explaining the physics
C · Stage shown: Hold 10 s

Saturday Cartoon

Chunky outlines, bright colours, big rounded text. Both checkmarks are lit and a fat green ring fills around the hub while the count runs down. The easiest style to read from the couch.

Feel
Friendly, celebratory
HUD
Big chips with checkmarks, giant countdown
Kid fit
Best of the four
D · Stage shown: Despin & tow

Ship Console

Amber phosphor on black with scanlines, like the Ranger's own flight display. A spin dial shows the rotation bleeding off, then the pair is pushed into the hatched safe zone on the right.

Feel
Retro, gritty, NASA
HUD
Gauges and dials, one colour only
Kid fit
Cool, but one colour makes states harder to tell apart

The docking sequence

  1. 1Approach

    Endurance starts near the top at a random spot in the middle 75%, spinning and drifting up. Ranger sits still at the bottom.

    Mockup A
  2. 2Match position

    Fly over the hub and match its drift. First indicator lights when you're close and moving at the same speed.

    Mockup B (lit)
  3. 3Match spin

    Spin up with L until the ship lines up with the ghost and turns at the same rate. Second indicator lights.

    Mockup B
  4. 4Hold 10 s

    Keep both matched while the clamps engage. Drift out and the timer resets.

    Mockup C
  5. 5Despin & tow

    Press K to slow the spin, then push the joined ships into the safe zone on the right.

    Mockup D

Pilot · position

Thrusters push along the screen, not the ship's nose. W always pushes up the screen, even while the ship is spinning. Let go and you keep coasting; press the opposite key to slow down.

K↺
L↻

Co-pilot · spin

Hold L to spin faster clockwise, K to spin faster counter-clockwise. Let go and the spin rate stays where it is. To dock, match Endurance's spin rate and line up with the ghost.

Pick a style (or mix them, like the cartoon look with the flight-plan ghost and trail) and I'll build the playable version from it.
