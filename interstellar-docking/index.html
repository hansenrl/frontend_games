      When you let go of a key

      Ease to a stop: let go and the Ranger settles alongside the station, matching its drift and spin. Once docked, the joined ships slow to a standstill.

      Spin is flown by

      Autopilot: the spin keys are switched off. It turns the Ranger to line up with the station and match its spin, then slows the spin once you dock, so one player can fly alone.
