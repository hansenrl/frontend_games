Art direction · round 2 · live canvas

Endurance Docking, Round Two

The Saturday Cartoon mockup won round one. Here are three more takes in that family, plus a papercraft look and a cel-shaded look. As before, each screen shows a different stage of docking, so the five together still cover the whole game.

Cartoon family

E · Stage shown: Match spin

Toybox

Shiny plastic toy colours, with each module a different bright capsule. Adds the dashed ghost Ranger from the flight-plan mockup and a pulsing arrow that tells the spin player which key to hold.

Feel
Playroom, bouncy
HUD
Toy buttons; spin chip has a mini meter with a green target
Kid fit
Very high, and the hints make it self-teaching
F · Stage shown: Approach

Comic Book

Halftone dots, ink outlines and a panel border. Explosions come with a KA-BOOM! and speed lines, and the Ranger talks in speech bubbles, which is a fun way to give instructions without a HUD.

Feel
Loud, dramatic, funny
HUD
Caption boxes and tick-boxes; hints as speech bubbles
Kid fit
High, if he enjoys reading the bubbles
G · Stage shown: Docked, now despin

Cute Crew

The Ranger has a face: big eyes in the cockpit that look where it's heading. Docking earns a ribbon, up to three stars (for a quick, steady dock) and confetti, then a friendly card says what to do next.

Feel
Warm, rewarding
HUD
Ribbon, star rating, a "next step" card with the key to press
Kid fit
Best for younger kids; the stars give replay value

Crafty & cel-shaded

H · Stage shown: Hold 10 s

Papercraft

A shoebox diorama: construction-paper sky, stars hanging on strings, cardboard modules, popsicle-stick spokes and a brass paper fastener for the hub. The Ranger is a folded paper plane. Smoke is cotton wool. Ten paper dots get stamped green during the hold.

Feel
Handmade, cosy, charming
HUD
Masking-tape labels, marker ticks, a swinging tag
Kid fit
High, and you could build a real one together
I · Stage shown: Despin & tow

Cel-Shaded

Flat colours with a hard-edged shadow that stays put while the station spins, so the rotation reads clearly. Swirly painted clouds, round smoke puffs with curls, and curly wind trails behind anything that moves.

Feel
Adventurous, storybook
HUD
Gold-trimmed panels; spin shown as pips that drain
Kid fit
High, and the prettiest when moving
